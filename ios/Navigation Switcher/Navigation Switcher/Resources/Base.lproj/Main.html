Almost there

Turn on the Safari extension once. It takes about a minute.

  1. Open Safari Settings

    Tap the button. The Settings app opens on Safari’s page.

    Open Safari Settings

    If Settings opens somewhere else, go back to the main Settings list and tap Apps, then Safari. On iOS 17 or earlier, tap Safari directly in Settings.

  2. Tap Extensions

    Scroll down to the General section in Safari’s settings and tap Extensions.

    Safari
    Profiles›
    Extensions›

  3. Turn on Navigation Switcher

    Tap Navigation Switcher and switch on Allow Extension.

    Navigation Switcher
    Allow Extension

  4. Allow the map websites

    Under Permissions, set every website in the list to Allow. Without this, links will not be switched.

    Permissions
    maps.apple.comAllow
    www.google.comAllow
    maps.app.goo.glAllow

  5. Pick your maps app

    Open Safari and tap the aA button in the address bar, then tap Navigation Switcher. Choose Apple Maps or Google Maps.

    Safari
    Show Reader
    Navigation Switcher›

That’s it. Open any maps link and it will open in your chosen app.
