# Privacy Policy

**Navigation Switcher**
Last updated: 2026-11-02

Navigation Switcher is a Safari extension that redirects maps links to your preferred maps app (Apple Maps or Google Maps). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## Summary

Navigation Switcher does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

## What the extension accesses

- **Maps links you open in Safari.** To redirect a link, the extension reads the address of pages on supported maps websites (such as Google Maps, `maps.app.goo.gl`, `goo.gl/maps` and `maps.apple.com`). It uses this only to build the equivalent link in your chosen maps app. Addresses are processed on your device and are never saved or sent to us.
- **Short links.** When you open a Google Maps short link (`maps.app.goo.gl` or `goo.gl/maps`), the extension makes a single request to that same short link to find out where it points. This request goes to the link's own server (Google) in the same way your browser would have requested the page anyway. We do not receive or see it.

The extension has no access to any other websites, your browsing history, your location, your contacts or any other data on your device.

## What is stored

Two values are saved locally on your device using Safari's extension storage:

- your selected maps app (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Off);
- a counter of how many links have been converted.

This data never leaves your device. Removing the app deletes it.

## Data sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone, because we do not collect any.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
